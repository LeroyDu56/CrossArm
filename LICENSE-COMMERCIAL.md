# Commercial licence

The [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE) that covers CrossArm grants
non-production use only. **Production use requires a commercial licence** from
the Licensor. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for what counts as production
use in practice.

A commercial licence lets you use CrossArm in production: on programs that run
on real robots, on conversions delivered to your customers, and as part of
billed migration work.

With it you receive a licence file, `crossarm.licence`: installed next to
`CrossArm.exe` or from the window, it replaces the evaluation mark in every
program CrossArm writes with your licence number and company name.

Terms are agreed case by case, because a one-robot changeover and a
multi-site migration are not the same job. Scope, duration, support and
pricing are set in the agreement.

To get a useful answer in one round trip, say roughly:

- how many robots, cells or sites are involved;
- whether the converted programs are delivered to your own customers;
- whether you need to keep your own modifications private.

Buying a commercial licence takes nothing away from the rights the Business
Source License already gives you.
